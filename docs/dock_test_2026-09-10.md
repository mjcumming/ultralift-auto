# Dock test — 2026-09-10

Live HydroHoist UltraLift UL2 8800, Cobalt aboard, device `boat-lift` at `192.168.4.44`. Operator at the dock (red Stop + manual valve overrides). Firmware on the boat during these runs was the ADR-015 build (Sep 9); winning-pulse and Guide (0.11.0) were **not** on the device yet.

Purpose: prove as much of the ADR-015 fail-safe as we could without inventing a hose-off, then record why we are adding a fourth height (**Guide**).

---

## Proven

### 1. Inject descent (`test_inject`)

`Test: inject level fail` from Lift, physically level. Same hard-stop path as a real list past Tilt Critical — the lie is only the trigger.

- First attempt on the then-current firmware sealed immediately at Lift (`descent timeout`). Same-tick unsigned wrap: supervisor had already sampled `now` before `start_emergency_descent` stamped `lower_start_time`. Fixed: timeout requires `now >= started` **and** elapsed > `Lower Timeout`.
- After that fix: `EMERGENCY descent: test_inject` → both valves open, blower off → sealed at Ready. List was still ~0%. Proves the **descent machinery**, not the detectors.

### 2. Cal-nudge hard stop (`level_divergence`)

Port Lift cal nudged −2.61° → +1.15° so the live pair looked like a real list. Hard stop fired, descent to Ready, seal. Port Lift cal restored to **−2.609253**.

### 3. Real one-valve dump

Operator opened Starboard by hand. Level Error hit **5.4%** immediately (~Tilt Critical 3° on the ~58.5° master span) → hard stop, **not** the 30 s rest-level keeper. List grew to ~7% then shrank; sealed at Ready (~4%, under 3°).

### 4. Second induce (wave on the 3° knife-edge)

Closed the valve around **3.5%**, but the list coasted to **~5.13%** in the same tick the persist window expired → hard stop again. Waves sit on the 3° edge; do not lower Tilt Critical.

### 5. In-move throttle

Raise from a Port-high **4.9%** list: `Leveling — holding Port back` → ~0% at arrival.

### 6. Rest-level vent (hole)

Parked Port-low ~**4.2%**. After 30 s persist: `Leveling — venting Starboard`, 10 s air cap, ended **−2.7%** (“winning, retry later”). Stuck in the 2–3% hysteresis hole — the 10 s clock stopped a pulse that was still shrinking. That is why parked pulses now keep going while winning (finish line = `Level Deadband` − hysteresis). **Needs OTA.**

### 7. Rest-level feed (true leveled)

Short Port crack, height **95%** (≤ 97% Lift Target, so `height_ok`). `Leveling — feeding Port`, **−3.1% → −0.8%**, height 95.2% → 96.2%. True **leveled** exit. Still Lifted.

---

## Not proven (do not claim)

- Hold the feed valve closed so a rest-level pulse **fails** → `level_correct_fail` descent.
- Residual list **>3° at Ready** → continue to Lowered + `emerg_lock`.
- Catch-up fail mid-move.
- IMU trust lost during descent.

## Why Lowered “ran away”

Lowered is one-sided: `angle > cal_lowered − zone_tol` (~50.3°). Arrival goes **`LOWERED_VENT`** — both valves stay **OPEN** (ADR-009). HOLD in that band re-opens the vents unless Stop latched them.

That is correct for “true bottom, keep dumping.” It is wrong for “boat floating, bunks still guiding the hull in the slip.” That morning that useful height was about **1–5% / 51–53°** — inside the Lowered zone — so Status became `Lowered — vent open` and the tanks kept emptying.

Ready (~13% / ~46°) is the other existing rest: boat **on** the bunks, sealed, walkable. Not a float.

---

## Decision: add Guide (ADR-016)

Fourth height: **boat floating, bunks still a slip centerline.** Web/HA go-to added the same day (0.11.1). No dock button. No firmware auto-trigger.

**Name: Guide.** Alternatives considered: Slip, Dock, Align, Center, Guided entry. Do **not** call it Float — `cal_angle_float` is already Ready.

Critical rule once captured: Stop at Guide **stays sealed**. Report Guide before Lowered when the zones overlap. Green Lowered LED off while in Guide. No fifth LED.

**No Guide angle was written today.** The lift was still moving or vents were open whenever we looked. Capture later: Stop-seal at the good height, then **Calibrate: set GUIDE** and **Calibrate Port: set GUIDE**.
